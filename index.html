Meet Helen & the Studio

Pebble Lane Art Studio is run by Helen, an art teacher, artist and facilitator based in Lordship, Dundalk, Co. Louth. With over 1,100 followers cheering on every class, camp and workshop, the studio has become a warm, creative home for children and adults alike across the local community.

Every session — whether it's a weekly children's class, a Halloween camp, or a relaxed evening of guided painting — is about spending time together, building confidence, and creating something to be proud of.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you cater for?
Children's classes and camps are generally suitable from age 6/7 up to 16, and parent & child sessions welcome children from age 7. Adult sessions are open to everyone — no experience needed!
Are materials included?
Yes — all materials are included in every class, camp, workshop and painting evening, along with light refreshments at parent & child and adult sessions.
How do I book a place?
The easiest way is to message Helen directly on WhatsApp at 087 654 8388. Spaces are limited across all classes and events, so early booking is always advised.
Do you run birthday parties?
Yes! Pebble Lane Art Studio hosts creative, mess-free birthday parties where every child leaves with their own artwork to take home.
